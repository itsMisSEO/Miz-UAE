## Legality of Betting in the UAE

UAE gambling law, rooted in Islamic law, carries heavy penalties: players can face up to 2 years in prison or a fine of AED 50,000, and operators risk up to 10 years in prison plus asset seizure. On top of that, Article 38 of the Cyber Crime Law sets fines of AED 250,000–500,000 for promoting unauthorized gambling.

The main risks for UAE users are:

- no consumer protection and no legal recourse under UAE law
- banking restrictions that make cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT) the main payment route
- personal responsibility for staying within the law
- disputes settled under foreign jurisdictions (Curacao, Malta, UK)
- no UAE licence or local regulatory oversight for any of these platforms

Domestic gambling is still banned and the ban is enforced. At the same time, there are early signs of careful liberalization, driven by tourism and economic diversification. Each user should assess their own legal exposure and take full responsibility before using any offshore platform.
